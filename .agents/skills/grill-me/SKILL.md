# Grill Me

One high-value owner conversation supplies the preference, business,
legal, privacy, and irreversible decisions needed for autonomous delivery.

## Prepare

1. Read `docs/preferences.md`, `docs/product/BRIEF.md`,
   `docs/product/EXPERIENCE.md`, and `docs/product/QUALITY.md`.
2. Inspect the repository and research implementation details first. Do not ask
   the owner to choose naming, file placement, architecture, tests, or an
   existing design token.
3. Remove questions already answered durably. Newer explicit owner direction
   supersedes stale prose.

## Ask one batch

Ask only genuine preference, business, legal, privacy, distribution, asset, or
irreversible technical forks. Group the smallest sufficient set under:

1. problem, audience, jobs, and measurable outcome;
2. must-have depth and explicit non-goals;
3. business model and distribution intent;
4. data sources, accounts, privacy, network/offline behavior, retention, and
   product-learning consent;
5. journeys, design references and anti-references, tone, density, motion,
   accessibility, and asset direction; and
6. release target, risk tolerance, fixed constraints, and production services.

For each fork, put the recommendation first and give two or three mutually
exclusive options with one consequence each. Prefer rendered variants for a
visual fork. Do not dribble follow-up questions that could have been included
in this batch.

## Persist the answer

After the owner responds:

1. update the four durable product files without inventing unanswered facts;
2. record cross-feature preferences in `docs/preferences.md` and feature-local
   decisions in the living plan;
3. mark unresolved items explicitly and apply the constitution's narrow
   escalation boundary; and
4. hand the durable inputs to contract negotiation. Do not start source work
   from conversational answers alone.

The intake is complete only when another fresh agent can reconstruct intended
users, product depth, experience direction, quality bar, privacy posture,
distribution intent, non-goals, and remaining escalations from committed files.

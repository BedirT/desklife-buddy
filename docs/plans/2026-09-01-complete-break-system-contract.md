# Complete break system contract - research and owner review plan

Status: blocked-owner
Intake/escalation status: clear

## Outcome

The owner receives one complete, evidence-linked design package for the entire break system:
signals, clock semantics, presets, suppression, every action and timeout, event logging,
adaptation, cross-device behavior, optional intelligence, privacy, performance, accessibility,
failure recovery, and recurring-surface delight. Every recommendation is labeled as retained,
proposed, rejected, or genuinely open. Production implementation does not begin until the owner
has reviewed and approved the package.

## Non-goals

- No production Swift, package, target, entitlement, permission, schema, or UI implementation.
- No production bundle identity, signing, CloudKit schema, App Store submission, or capability
  activation.
- No silent promotion of research proposals into canonical product decisions.
- No claim that location, HealthKit, Watch, input, app identity, a timer, or a local model proves
  presence, gaze, sitting, movement, productivity, or a health outcome.

## Open escalations - clear before step 1

- None. The owner explicitly authorized comprehensive research, parallel specialist work, and a
  Claude critique, while reserving implementation approval until after reviewing all findings and
  proposed decisions.

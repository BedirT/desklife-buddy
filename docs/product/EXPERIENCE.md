# Experience direction

Status: `accepted`
Intake revision: 1
Authority: `design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `design/INTERACTION_SPEC.md`,
`design/SCREEN_INVENTORY.md`, and `design/COPY_INVENTORY.md`

## Core journey

- Entry and first success: onboarding requests no permissions; the no-permission path works.
- Primary repeated workflow: nearly silent status, quiet heads-up, graceful self-running
  break, immediate Escape, and optional acknowledgement.
- Recovery and edge states: suppression explains itself; missed opportunities merge rather
  than stack; refusal never increases loudness.
- Desktop expectations: menu-bar presence, Dock fallback, native windows, per-display break
  panels, full keyboard operation, and explicit multi-display/focus restoration.

## Design DNA

- Desired qualities: calm, confident, polished, warm, private, precise, quiet, intentional,
  and unmistakably macOS-native.
- References: Horizon means sky holds one instruction, ground holds stable controls, and the
  horizon carries progress; use native behavior without losing that meaning.
- Anti-references: medical, fitness-gamified, corporate, childish, spiritual-cliche,
  cyberpunk, AI-generated, generic dashboard, or enlarged phone UI.
- Information density: Level 2 has one instruction, one timer, obvious exits, no dashboard.
- Visual direction: only centralized design-system tokens/components; light/dark and all
  accessibility appearances are first-class.

## Voice and behavior

- Copy voice: approved strings ship exactly from `design/COPY_INVENTORY.md`.
- Tone: invitations, estimates, and user declarations; never shame, verification, diagnosis,
  prevention, or implied body sensing.
- Motion: only the closed list in `design/INTERACTION_SPEC.md`; no loops, pulses, flashing,
  shimmer, rapid zoom, parallax, or continuously animated countdown.
- Accessibility: all applicable §K blocks in `design/SCREEN_INVENTORY.md` are acceptance
  tests; Escape always works and alternative movement paths have equal semantic dignity.

## Assets

- Provisional asset direction: use only sources and owed-state guidance in the handoff.
- Final asset boundary: owed/provisional assets are not final art. Build no-image/no-motion
  plumbing for static mode but do not invent its visuals.

## Experience non-goals

- Deliberately excluded patterns: all prohibited product, visual, and copy patterns in root
  `CLAUDE.md`.
- Unresolved owner escalations: items in `design/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`; keep provisional choices
  configurable rather than silently settling them.

## Product

- Problem: adult computer users need calm reminders to interrupt long runs of estimated
  active computer use; alternatives are often intrusive, medically overconfident,
  inaccessible, or productivity-oriented.
- Audience: adult Mac users; not medical patients, children, employer-compliance programs,
  or anyone seeking body/posture monitoring.
- Jobs/outcome: remember distinct visual and movement opportunities without fighting
  workflow, then understand weekly patterns without scores or grades.
- First version: the complete build order in `design/HANDOFF.md`.
- Non-goals: every prohibited pattern in root `CLAUDE.md` and the handoff.
- Business/distribution: unsettled; no purchase or distribution work is authorized.

## Data and risk

- Sources: bounded local input-event recency, awake/unlocked/session state, user
  declarations, and optional ephemeral exclusion or busy/free context. No content capture.
- Accounts/network: core is entirely local, with no account and no network.
- Privacy: `research/health/07_PRIVACY_AND_DATA_SPEC.md` is authoritative; analytics is off
  by default and optional permissions are requested only at moment of value.
- Product-learning adapters: none.
- Risk: conservative on claims, autonomy, accessibility, privacy, and data. Never infer a
  body state or health outcome from computer activity, idle, or a timer.

## Experience

- Journey: quiet ambient status, heads-up, dismissible break invitation, optional
  acknowledgement, and user-initiated weekly reflection.
- Direction: Horizon in `design/`; category references inform native adaptation, never cloning.
- Voice/craft: calm, warm, precise, desktop-native, low-cognitive-load, full keyboard and
  VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast, and equal-status movement paths.
- Art: owed assets and approval gaps remain in the handoff; do not invent static-mode visuals.

## Delivery

- Target/channel: macOS first; distribution channel is unsettled.
- Constraints: native macOS, macOS 26+, Swift 6.2 harness baseline, no invasive entitlements,
  and no identity rename yet.
- Paid/hosted services: none authorized.
- Escalations: product identity, signing/distribution, final-art rights, and open questions
  carried in the handoff.

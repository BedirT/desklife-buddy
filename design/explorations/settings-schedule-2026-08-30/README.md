## Design brief

### Keep

- Three truthful rhythm choices and their distinct approved ceramic ribbon stages.
- Balanced as the current selection.
- Independent visual-reset and movement clocks.
- Timing, Delivery, and Advanced schedules behavior.
- Native macOS density, keyboard behavior, localization, and accessibility.

### Change

- Reduce the oversized preset cards and card pile.
- Make Timing and Delivery scan as one aligned system.
- Use the 1000 by 700 width efficiently while retaining a clear 780-point collapse.
- Make the page calm enough for repeated use without hiding important controls.

### Remove

- Empty vertical gaps that delay useful controls.
- Competing container borders and dashboard-like grouping.
- Decorative material effects that fight the accepted solid shell.

## Directions

- `direction-a-comparison-form.png`: compact equal-rank preset comparison followed by one
  continuous native form. Lowest interaction novelty and the quietest default.
- `direction-b-dual-lane.png`: a coarse two-lane schedule map makes the independent clocks
  explicit. Informative, but risks reading as analytics.
- `direction-c-current-rhythm.png`: one selected-rhythm summary leads, with the alternatives
  reduced below it. Strongest current-state hierarchy, but uses more vertical space.
- `direction-d-split-editor.png`: compact preset list beside a current-schedule editor. Efficient
  at wide sizes, but must avoid reading as a second sidebar and needs a clean compact collapse.
